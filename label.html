The label

Wrap your body
in art.

Australian photographer and fashion designer nikkib is a lifestyle brand inspired by raw and real landscapes, people and style from around the world, each piece foretelling its own original story.

Photography
meets fabric.

Our exclusive line of stunning dual season apparel offers a quality and design that sets it distinctly apart from the everyday. The images that breathe life into the fabric that is the nikkib identity are lovingly taken, unique to the label, and intended to inspire.

The art works are printed onto a sustainable and environmentally friendly textile, spun from cellulose fibres taken from beech trees, with a texture very similar to silk.

The nikkib philosophy is simple: wrap your body in art.
