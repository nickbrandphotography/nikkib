The label

nikkib is a lifestyle brand inspired by raw and real landscapes, people and style from around the world, each piece foretelling its own original story.

Our exclusive line of stunning dual season apparel offers a quality and design that sets it distinctly apart from the everyday. The images that breathe life into the fabric are lovingly taken, unique to the label, and intended to inspire.

The art works are printed onto a sustainable and environmentally friendly textile, spun from cellulose fibres taken from beech trees, with a texture very similar to silk.

  • 24pieces, each its own photograph
  • 6regions, from Sydney to Burma
  • 100×200centimetres, dual season
  • 100%Modal, from beech trees
